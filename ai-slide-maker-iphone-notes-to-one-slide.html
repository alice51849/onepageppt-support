From notes to one slide in three steps

  1. Add anything: type, paste, snap a photo, or import a PDF or spreadsheet.
  2. Tap Make My Slide: your content is summarised into headlines and turned into the right visual.
  3. Present it, or export it.

There is no template picking and no dragging boxes around. The result is a single, presentation-ready 16:9 slide.

Redesign and edit

Six one-tap redesigns — Cleaner, More Visual, More Professional, More Bold, More Compact and More Editorial — each rebuild the slide with different chart forms, typography and layout. Lock any element you like and redesign around it. Tap any element to rewrite it, swap an image, reorder blocks or change the style, and use the full version history to go back to any earlier draft.

Frequently asked questions

What does an AI slide maker do?

It reads your raw material, finds the key points, summarises them into headlines and lays them out as a slide with a visual that fits the content, so you do not pick a template or drag boxes around.

Can it make a slide from a PDF or a screenshot?

Yes. OnePage PPT accepts typed notes, pasted text, screenshots, photos, PDFs and CSV tables, and turns them into a single 16:9 slide.

Will the AI invent numbers for my chart?

No. Its App Store listing says it only draws a chart the data can honestly support and never invents figures.

Can I open the slide in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides?

Yes. It exports an editable .pptx for PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides, plus PDF and high-resolution PNG.

Does it upload my notes to a server?

No. Everything runs on your device, with no account, no sign-in and no tracking, and it works with no network at all.

Is it a subscription?

No. The App Store listing says Lifetime Pro is a single purchase, with no subscription and no recurring fee.
