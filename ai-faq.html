Questions and answers

What is OnePage PPT?

An iPhone and iPad app (App Store ID 6798814385) that turns typed notes, pasted text, screenshots, photos, PDFs and CSV tables into one presentation-ready 16:9 slide. It finds the key points, summarises them into headlines and picks a fitting visual. There is no template to pick and no boxes to drag around.

Who is it for?

Consultants, founders, students, teachers, project managers and anyone who has to make one clear page out of a lot of information.

How is it paid for?

You can start for free. Lifetime Pro is a single one-time purchase, with no subscription and no recurring fee.

How is it different from other presentation apps?

It makes one slide instead of a multi-slide deck, and it chooses the visual from your content: numbers become column, bar, line, donut or funnel charts; steps become a process flow; dates become a timeline; two options become a comparison; findings become a matrix or a hierarchy. It only draws a chart the data can honestly support and never invents figures. Six one-tap redesigns (Cleaner, More Visual, More Professional, More Bold, More Compact, More Editorial) rebuild the slide, and you can lock any element you like.

What can I export?

An editable .pptx for PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides, a PDF for sharing and printing, and a high-resolution PNG for docs, email and chat.

Is my content private?

Everything runs on your device. Projects and images stay on your iPhone or iPad, and exported files leave it only when you share them yourself. No account, no sign-in, no tracking, and it works with no network at all.
